# Prompts for the kit pictures

One prompt per kit, describing the real kit you hear, plus a style to add to each so all three come out
as one set. They're seen from the drummer's seat (hi-hat on the left), like the kit view and the default
mix: a drum on the left of the picture is on the left of what you hear.

Once there's a picture for a kit, its clickable outlines get traced on it by hand (each drum and cymbal
mapped to the kit's own recordings), so each needs every piece the prompt names, no more and no fewer.

## Style (add to the end of each prompt)

Photorealistic studio photograph, taken from the drummer's seat slightly above head height, looking
forward and down at about 35 degrees at the whole kit. Right-handed setup: the hi-hat and its pedal on
the left, the ride on the right. Every drum and cymbal fully in the frame, clearly separate, none hidden
behind another. The kit stands in a dark studio, lit from above by soft warm light with a faint rim
light, the background falling away to near black. Sharp focus everywhere, no depth-of-field blur. No
people, no hands, no drumsticks, no microphones or cables, no text, no logos, no brand names, no
watermark. Wide 16:9 framing, 2560 x 1440.

Negative prompt, for generators that take one: people, hands, drumsticks, microphones, cables, text,
logo, watermark, blur, cropped cymbals, extra drums, missing drums, warped hardware.

## Crocell

A modern metal drum kit with matte black shells and chrome hardware. One bass drum in the centre with a
double pedal (two beaters, two footboards) at the bottom. A deep bronze-coloured metal snare drum with a
white coated head in front, just left of centre. Two rack toms with clear heads above the bass drum, the
smaller on the left. Two floor toms with clear heads on the right, the larger at the front right. A
hi-hat and eight cymbals: on the left, the hi-hat low beside the snare, a large crash high above it, and
a dark traditional-finish china at the far left; above the rack toms, two small splash cymbals side by
side; a crash high at the top centre; a large brilliant-finish crash just right of centre; an upside-down
china high on the right; and a big ride low at the far right, over the floor toms. A mix of brilliant
polished gold-bronze cymbals and darker traditional ones.

## DRS

A handmade vintage-style drum kit in a glossy orange marine-pearl wrap, with chrome hardware and white
coated heads. One bass drum in the centre with a single pedal, its white head facing the camera. A snare
drum with a light metal shell and a white coated head in front, left of centre. One small rack tom on
the bass drum, on the left. Two floor toms on the right, the larger at the front right. A hi-hat and
three cymbals, all bright, golden and finely lathed: the hi-hat low on the left, a crash high on the
left, a large crash high just right of centre, and a ride lower on the right, over the floor toms. The
kit stands on a red Persian rug.

## Muldjord

The first try came back with a second floor tom and a third crash, so this one counts every piece.
`docs/muldjord-picture-prompt.txt` has it ready to paste, with the style and the list of things to
avoid already in it. Its style leaves out "the ride on the right", because this kit has two rides.

A big double-bass metal drum kit with glossy black lacquer shells and chrome hardware, its toms and
cymbals held on a curved chrome drum rack. Exactly seven drums and exactly six cymbals counting the
hi-hat, nothing more, nothing less.

The seven drums: two bass drums side by side in the centre, each with its own pedal at the bottom; one
chrome snare drum with a white coated head in front, left of centre; three rack toms in a row across
the top of the two bass drums, smallest on the left; and one large floor tom at the front right. Only
one floor tom.

The six cymbals, from left to right: a ride at the far left, beyond the hi-hat; the hi-hat low on the
left, beside the snare; a crash high on the left; a crash high just right of centre; a china high on
the right; and a large ride low at the far right, over the floor tom. Only two crashes, no splash.
Bronze cymbals in brilliant and darker traditional finishes.
